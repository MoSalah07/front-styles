Four more brands ship alongside `crimson`: `ocean` (product blue), `ember` (commerce orange), `forest` (deep green) and `slate` (cool grey). Each is one JSON seed compiled into full OKLCH ramps and light/dark semantic maps, and each clears every audited WCAG AA text pair in both modes.

`SettingsPage` offers all six brands by default and wraps its brand toggles instead of overflowing on narrow cards.
